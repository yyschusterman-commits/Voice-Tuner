Settings

Octave guard helps when the tuner shows the right note one octave off. Turn it off to see exactly what it hears. Raise sensitivity if quiet notes show “can’t read it”; lower it if room noise shows up as notes.

10-minute test

  1. With no headphones, play the tone next to the mic with the mic on. The tuner should read the target within a few cents. That checks your mic path.
  2. Sing a comfortable low, middle and high note. Does it show the right note and octave?
  3. Hold a steady “ooh”. Does the needle stay fairly still?
  4. Slide your voice up, then down. Does the needle move the right way?
  5. Stay silent in a quiet room. It should say “listening…” and show no note.
  6. Is the example tone easy to copy? Try both sounds.
