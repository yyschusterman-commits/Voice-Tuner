How to get a plan, and what's in it

Copy this, fill in the song, and send it to Claude:

Make a Pitch Hold song plan (format pitchhold-plan-1) for "SONG" by ARTIST. Easy level.

A plan lists the tempo, beats per bar, sections and the chords in each bar. Bars are split with |. Two chords in one bar split it evenly, and C:3 G:1 sets beats yourself. - holds the last chord and x is silence. Each section can set drums (none, pop, rock, half, four, hiphop, waltz), bass (pulse, hold, eighths, off) and pad (hold, stabs, off).

{
  "format": "pitchhold-plan-1",
  "title": "Example", "artist": "Me", "level": "easy",
  "bpm": 100, "beatsPerBar": 4, "key": "C",
  "sections": {
    "intro":  { "chords": "C | G | Am | F", "drums": "none" },
    "chorus": { "chords": "F G | Em Am | F G | C", "drums": "pop", "bass": "pulse" }
  },
  "order": ["intro", "chorus", "chorus"]
}

Recording settings

Song

“Check the MIDI” plays the file exactly as written, in its real octaves, with simple stand-in sounds and the melody included. If that doesn’t sound like the song, the MIDI file is the problem; try another one.

0:00

Piano roll

Solid = your recording · gold outline = borrowed from a nearby note · hollow = stand-in sound. Notes slide left and play as they reach the keys. Click or drag on the keys to hear your sound for any note (the drum names play your beatbox). Click a part’s color to mute or unmute it. Click a note for details.

Parts

Each part of the MIDI file plays with one of your sounds. Octave picks which of your notes it uses; “Pitch up” plays them higher than you sang, like a synth, for parts that were too high to sing. Set the lead melody to “You sing it” and record it below. Turn off parts that double the melody if it gets crowded. Changes apply from the next loop.

Your vocals

Wear headphones, press Record vocals and sing along. The song starts from the top and stops at the end, or when you press Stop.

Mix

Autotune moves each held note toward its exact pitch: Light about halfway, Medium most of the way, Heavy all the way. Your recordings never change, so you can switch any time. Tempo, key and part changes apply from the next loop.
